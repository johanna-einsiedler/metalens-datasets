# MASEMiner evaluation · Video gaming and body mass (Marker et al., 2022)

Ten primary studies of the meta-analysis by Marker, Gnambs & Appel (2022) on sedentary video gaming and body mass, extracted with the MASEMiner direct-information preset (gpt-5.6-sol) and then corrected cell by cell to the reference database of the meta-analysis. Each correction is a recorded human edit, so the published values equal the reference coding and the audit trail shows the model's omissions, over-extractions and wrong values. Held-out validation case of Schroeders, Gnambs & Einsiedler (2026).
